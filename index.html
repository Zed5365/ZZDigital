▶ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What is a "web site"?
A: A web site is a collection of electronic pages stored on a computer called a server. Anyone in the world with a modem can look at it. It is like a brochure, but on the computer, and it never runs out.

Q: How long does it take?
A: Most home pages take 2–3 weeks. Frames add one week.

Q: Do I need my own domain name?
A: Not necessarily, but they are only $70 for two years from InterNIC and it looks much more professional than a tilde in your address.

Q: Will my site work on a Macintosh?
A: Yes. We test on both platforms.

Q: What about the Year 2000 problem?
A: All ZZDigital web sites are certified Y2K COMPLIANT. Your site will keep working on January 1st, 2000. Guaranteed.
